# Compatibility Matrix

| Release | Minimum NetBox Version | Maximum NetBox Version | netbox-branching | Python |
|---------|------------------------|------------------------|------------------|--------|
| 0.4.x   | 4.6.9                  | 4.6.99                 | 1.1.3 - 1.1.x    | 3.12+  |
| 0.5.x   | 4.7.0                  | 4.7.99                 | 1.2.x            | 3.12+  |

Releases before 0.4.0 are not listed and are not recommended. Use 0.4.x on NetBox 4.6 and 0.5.x on NetBox 4.7.
